# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People establishing nonprofits or businesses, nonprofit leaders seeking guidance, and organizations booking Dr. Wesley Proctor for speaking or training. They need to understand his services, choose the right next step, and reach him from a phone or desktop.

## Product Purpose

Present Wesley Proctor Enterprise's services and Dr. Proctor's expertise with credibility, and make inquiries, bookings, and existing service payments easy to find. Preserve the existing GoDaddy-compatible PHP and HTML routes.

## Brand Personality

Professional and enterprise-oriented, as confirmed by the owner. Dr. Proctor is a consultant. Preserve the existing business content and change its presentation. Use actual photography and a clear, confident tone.

## Anti-references

The owner rejected the current site's appearance. Avoid its busy composite hero, dense centered text, inconsistent navigation, generic template content, and nonfunctional newsletter UI. Never invent testimonials, affiliations, prices, or availability.

## Design Principles

1. Let Dr. Proctor's actual photography and work establish trust.
2. Give visitors one understandable next step at each decision.
3. Make the service differences easy to scan.
4. Keep contact and booking accessible on every device.
5. Preserve existing URLs and actual business details.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, semantic navigation, labeled forms, reduced-motion support, and responsive layouts. Automated checks are supporting evidence, not complete accessibility certification.
