# Wesley Proctor Enterprise visual system

Visitors often arrive on a phone in daylight, looking for a credible person to help turn their idea into an organization. The site should feel welcoming, assured, and clear.

## Direction

A confident personal consultancy: crisp oversized sans typography, real portrait photography, generous pale surfaces, and a committed deep teal hero. Reference lane: the clarity of university executive-education sites with the warmth of a personal practice, not an editorial magazine.

## Colors

Deep teal: oklch(0.30 0.055 198). Teal action: oklch(0.42 0.070 198). Ink: oklch(0.23 0.025 198). Paper: oklch(0.985 0.005 95). Pale neutral: oklch(0.96 0.012 95). Warm chartreuse accent: oklch(0.88 0.11 112). Use chartreuse sparingly on dark surfaces.

## Typography

Public Sans for legibility and grounded executive-education character. Fluid 48–92px home display, 34–64px page headings, 18px body. Body text stays within 65 characters. Fallback Helvetica/sans-serif.

## Layout and components

1240px maximum content width; fluid 20–64px margins. Split portrait hero, numbered service rows, distinct booking band, concise contact footer. Buttons have simple 4px corners, strong contrast, and clear labels. No shadow-heavy cards or decorative icon grids.

## Behavior

Native disclosure navigation for services and booking; accessible mobile menu with escape dismissal. No entrance animation that hides content. Respect reduced motion. PHP service forms retain their existing field names and routes.
